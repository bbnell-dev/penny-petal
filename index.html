How to use Penny Petal

Your budget at a glance

Here is what each little money petal is tracking for you.

IncomeAll the money that has come in this month.
SpendingPurchases and payments you enter as transactions.
BillsWhat you expect to owe this month. When you pay one, add it as a transaction.
Left to spendIncome left after the transactions you have entered.
In savingsYour saved money. Set a savings goal here to track your progress.

Reading your graphs

The charts help you spot patterns without doing the math in your head.

Six month trend Shows income, spending, bills, and savings over time so you can see progress or changes.
Category donut Shows where money is going this month. Tap a category to see the entries inside it.
